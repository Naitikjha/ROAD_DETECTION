Explainable AI (XAI) Attribution & Error Analysis

Grad-CAM computes the gradient of the predicted class score with respect to each feature map channel in the final convolutional layer (self.last_conv). The channels are weighted via global average pooling of gradients, highlighting regions essential to decision making.
